Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 1, 2026

HeartNote AI is a writing and reflection app. This policy explains what information the app uses and how it is handled.

Information We Collect

When you create an account, we may collect your username, email address, hashed password, optional avatar image, and saved writing history. If you use the writing tools, the text you enter and the generated output may be stored so you can view your recent writings.

How We Use Information

We use this information to provide account access, save your writings, display your profile, generate writing responses, improve app reliability, and protect the service from misuse.

AI Writing

Text entered into the writing tools is used to generate responses. Avoid entering passwords, financial details, medical records, or other sensitive personal information into the writing prompts.

Local Desktop App

The Microsoft Store desktop app opens the HeartNote AI web experience in a secure Electron window. The desktop app itself does not sell personal data or add third-party advertising trackers.

Cookies And Sessions

HeartNote AI may use cookies or session storage to keep you signed in and protect account access.

File Uploads

If avatar upload is enabled, your selected image may be stored and used only as your profile avatar. If upload storage is unavailable, the app may show a default fallback avatar.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may use hosting, storage, or AI service providers only as needed to operate the app.

Data Deletion

You may delete saved writings or delete your account from inside the app when those controls are available. Account deletion removes your account data from the app database.

Children's Privacy

HeartNote AI is not intended for children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact the developer through the Microsoft Store listing.

Changes

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. The updated date at the top of this page will show when changes were made.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer through the Microsoft Store app listing or the support contact provided with the app.
